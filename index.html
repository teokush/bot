How the pick is made. For the trade type you choose, the scanner tries every side (e.g. Over 0–8 and Under 1–9), every entry digit and every market. It keeps only entries that also hold up on the last 30% of ticks (which they were not picked from), then ranks them by how unlikely their hit count is under pure chance. Because it checks hundreds of combinations, some look good by luck, so each pick is compared with the best score luck alone would give (Signal: Strong, Possible or Likely luck). Deriv synthetic digits are generated randomly, so past frequencies and entry digits describe the scanned sample and do not predict the next digit. In my simulations on random data, entries that scored well in the sample earned nothing on fresh data. Analysis only, not financial advice.
